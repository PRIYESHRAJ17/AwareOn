# AwareOn Study Area

Platform Scope: Northeast India (NER)

Primary Prototype Region: Sikkim

Primary Demonstration Focus: Gangtok / East Sikkim

Approach:
The prototype will use Sikkim as the initial modelling and validation region while the AwareOn platform architecture remains extensible to the wider NER.

Historical Event Strategy:
Use documented Sikkim landslide events and align available rainfall, terrain, remote-sensing and contextual evidence for historical replay and evaluation.
